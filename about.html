About Shaner Auto Detailing

We don't shy away from the tough jobs — we take pride in them.

The story

Shaner Auto Detailing LLC is an owner-operated shop in Owatonna, Minnesota, built on one belief: whether it's your daily driver or your hardest-working equipment, it deserves a level of care most people have never seen.

Owner Brandon Shaner is a certified paint-correction and ceramic-coating specialist who does the work himself — every vehicle gets the owner's standard, start to finish. He treats every car like it's headed to a car show — because you paid for it, and it's parked in your driveway.

From farm trucks to Harleys, from daily commuters to show-ready classics — we've done it all, and we take pride in the tough ones.

Straight talk

The honest answer

Sometimes the best service is telling you what you don't need. If the clear coat is gone, a wax isn't the fix — we'll tell you what we'd actually do, and what it would cost, before we touch your vehicle.
