Questions we hear a lot

How long does a detail take?

Every job is different — light cleans are the quickest, full details and coatings take longer. We'll give you an exact window when you book.

Do you work on trucks and farm equipment?

Absolutely — we don't shy away from the tough jobs. Daily drivers, trucks, SUVs, motorcycles, and your hardest-working equipment all get the same standard.

What products do you use?

Professional-grade products chosen for Minnesota conditions — road salt, hard water, and everything in between. Ask us about the right protection for your vehicle.
